# Mouse photos

`CrayonGrey.png`, `JetBlack.png` and `Ghost.png` are Lofree's top-down product photos of the Hypace in its three colourways, from [lofree.co](https://www.lofree.co/), cut out of their background. `build.ps1` builds them into `HypaceTray.exe` for the **Realistic** mouse style, so the program never needs to download them.

**The photos are © Lofree. They are not covered by this repository's MIT license**, and neither are the pictures of the realistic mouse made from them (in `docs/images/`).
