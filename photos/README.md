# Mouse photos

`CrayonGrey.png`, `JetBlack.png` and `Ghost.png` are Lofree's top-down product photos of the Hypace in its three colourways, from [lofree.co](https://www.lofree.co/), cut out of their background. `build.ps1` builds them into `HypaceTray.exe` for the **Realistic** mouse style, so the program never needs to download them. The program's icon (`src/app.ico`, made by `tools/make-icon.ps1`) is drawn from them too.

**The photos are © Lofree. They are not covered by this repository's MIT license**, and neither is anything made from them: the pictures of the realistic mouse (in `docs/images/`) and the program icon (`src/app.ico`).
